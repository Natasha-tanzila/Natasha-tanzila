## 🧠 About Me

I am an **AI researcher and lecturer** with a strong background in **computer science and engineering**, currently pursuing an **MPhil in Information Systems at Queensland University of Technology (QUT)**.

My work focuses on **human-centric and emotion-aware AI systems**, particularly at the intersection of:

- **Natural Language Processing (NLP) & Large Language Models (LLMs)**
- **Agentic AI and decision-support systems**
- **Healthcare, mental health & assistive technologies**
- **Wearables, IoT, augmented reality & applied machine learning**

I have experience across the **entire research-to-deployment pipeline** — from **ethical data collection and modelling** to **real-world system development** — and I actively publish in **peer-reviewed journals, conferences, and book chapters**.
